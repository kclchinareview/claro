about aurio ✨

a gentle, free toolkit for students and curious minds

aurio is a 100% free, non‑profit website built with love by a girl learning tech. Every tool — from the task breaker to the breathing bubble — is designed to be clear, calming, and accessible for everyone, especially neurodivergent students.

No ads, no tracking, no hidden costs. Just a warm corner of the internet where you can organise your thoughts, take a mindful break, or roll a dice for a little nudge.

💻 This project is also my personal portfolio as I learn web development. Every line of code is a small step forward — and I'm grateful you're here.

📱 add aurio to your home screen (like an app)

You can install this website as a web app! It will appear on your phone’s home screen and open like a native app.

🍏 iOS (iPhone / iPad)

1️⃣
Open aurio in Safari (not Chrome on iOS).
2️⃣
Tap the Share icon (square with arrow at the bottom of the screen).
3️⃣
Scroll down and tap “Add to Home Screen”.
4️⃣
Edit the name (e.g., “aurio”) and tap “Add”. The icon will appear on your home screen.

🤖 Android (Chrome / Samsung Internet)

1️⃣
Open aurio in Chrome or Samsung Internet.
2️⃣
Tap the menu (three dots) in the top‑right corner.
3️⃣
Select “Add to Home screen”.
4️⃣
Confirm the name and tap “Install”. The app icon will be added to your home screen.

✨ Once installed, aurio will launch in its own window, without browser toolbars — just like a real app.
